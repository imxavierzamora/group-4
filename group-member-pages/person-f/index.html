Fernando Haro

Fernando Haro is a Graduate Student at UC Berkeley with nearing five years of professional journalism experience.

Five Years

As my five-year anniversary of working in journalism approaches, I have been reflecting on the path that led me to this career and the experiences that have shaped me along the way. My journey into journalism has been a winding one, filled with unexpected twists and turns, but it has ultimately been a rewarding and fulfilling experience.

I've covered harrowing stories that have physically and emotionally drained me, but have also given me a sense of purpose and fulfillment.

I've always felt journalism could be exploitive to communities but it can also be a powerful tool to amplify the voices of those silenced by systemic oppression.

Now, as I near my five-year anniversary of my first job in journalism, I'm finding myself with one foot in and one foot out.

On one note, I'd love to continue in the industry and make a positive impact with my work. But the work I've been producing hasn't been the most fulfilling of my career.

I find myself happiest when I can pass one my experiences in the industry to my friends and members of my cohort.

That has only reassured me that I want to teach journalism someday.

It's why I'm at UC Berkeley. But I find myself wondering about the butterfly effects that could come with me stepping away from the hustle and bustle of a newsroom
